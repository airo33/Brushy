# Brushy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE YOU PUBLISH]
**Contact:** [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS]

Brushy ("the app") is an alarm and habit app that helps you brush your teeth. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **you can use Brushy without an account, and then everything stays on your phone. If you create an account, a small backup of your streak, alarms and settings is stored online. Photos are never uploaded to us. We don't sell or share your data.**

## Using Brushy without an account

If you don't create an account, the app stores the following only on your phone:

- **Alarm times and settings**: the times you set, your brushing length, daily goal and alarm sound.
- **Brushing history**: which brushes you completed and when, used to show your streaks.
- **Photos**: the app takes a photo at the start and the end of each brushing session as proof. The photos are saved in the app's private storage on your phone and shown in the "Brush photos" row. The app keeps the 60 most recent sessions and deletes older photos automatically.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

## If you create an account

Creating an account is optional. It lets you keep your streak if you change phones. When you create an account, we collect:

- **Your email address and a password.** The password is stored in a protected (hashed) form by our account provider, so neither we nor anyone else can read it.
- **A backup of your alarm times, settings and brushing history** (which brushes you completed and on which days), linked to your account.

**Photos are never uploaded to us.** They are stored only on your phone, even when you are signed in. (See "Your phone's own backups" below.)

## Friends and leaderboard (optional)

If you are signed in, you can choose to create a friend profile. This is separate from having an account, and **nothing is shared until you create it**. When you do, we store:

- **A display name** you choose (2 to 20 characters). Pick a name you are comfortable sharing, and avoid using your real full name if you prefer.
- **A friend code**, a random 8-character code that others can use to send you a friend request. People cannot find you by email.
- **Your streak numbers**: your current streak, your best streak, how many times you brushed in the last 7 days, and the most recent days you brushed or met your daily goal.

These are visible **only to people you have accepted as friends**, and they see only your display name and those numbers. Friends never see your email, photos, alarm times or settings. A friend request must be accepted before anything is visible, and a declined request cannot be re-sent by the same person. You can remove a friend or stop sharing at any time: Friends → "Stop sharing and delete my friend profile" deletes your profile and all of your friendships. Deleting your account also deletes them.

**Blocking and reporting.** You can block anyone you have a friend request or friendship with. Blocking removes the friendship and stops them from sending you requests, and they are not told. We store the list of people you have blocked so the block keeps working, and you can unblock someone at any time. You can also report a person for an inappropriate name, harassment, spam or another reason, with an optional note of up to 500 characters. A report stores who sent it, who it is about (including the name they had at the time), the reason and your note. Reports are used only to review and act on abuse. We do not tell the reported person who reported them. If you delete your account, reports you sent are deleted, while a report about someone is kept with their name even if they delete theirs, so abuse can still be handled.

Account data is stored with Supabase, our account and database provider, which processes it on our behalf. It is protected so that each account can only read and change its own data. We use your email only to run your account (for example, to send a confirmation link or a password reset code). We don't send marketing email.

**Signing out** keeps your backup in your account and clears the synced data (alarms, settings and history) from the phone. Photos stay on the phone.

**Deleting your account.** You can delete your account inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login and your backup from our servers. Data and photos already on your phone are not affected.

## Your phone's own backups

Your phone may back up its apps and their data, for example to iCloud on an iPhone or to Google's backup on Android, depending on your device settings. If that is turned on, the data the app stores on your phone, including your brushing photos, may be included in that backup. That backup is created and controlled by Apple or Google under your own account and settings. We do not receive it and cannot access it. You can manage or turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Camera

The app asks for camera access so it can take the photos described above. Camera images are not uploaded. **[KEEP ONLY WHEN THE CAMERA AI SHIPS:]** While you brush, the camera is also analyzed on your device to detect a toothbrush and your face, so the app can tell you are really brushing. This analysis happens entirely on your phone, and no images leave it.

## Notifications

The app asks for permission to send notifications so it can remind you to brush. These are local notifications scheduled on your phone. The app does not use remote push notifications.

## Network use

Without an account, the app does not send your data anywhere. **[KEEP ONLY WHEN THE CAMERA AI SHIPS:]** The machine-learning model used to check your brushing is built into the app, so using it requires no download or network connection. With an account, the app communicates with Supabase to sign you in and sync the data described above.

## Analytics, advertising and third parties

The app contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking**. We do not sell your data. The only third party that handles your data is Supabase, and only if you create an account. If you use the friends feature, friends you have accepted can see the limited numbers described above.

## Your choices

- Turn off the camera or notifications at any time in your phone's Settings. The app still opens, but photo proof and reminders won't work.
- You can use the app without an account.
- Delete your account in the app at any time (see above). Deleting the app from your phone removes the data stored on the phone, including photos. Copies in your phone's own backup, if you have one, are managed through your phone's settings (see "Your phone's own backups"). If you have an account, your backup stays until you delete the account.

## Children

Brushy is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
